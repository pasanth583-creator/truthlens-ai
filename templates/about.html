1. Transformer Language Model (DistilBERT)

At the classification core is a fine-tuned distilbert-base-uncased transformer model trained across multi-domain datasets (including benchmark ISOT and LIAR corpora). The classifier predicts three veracity categories:

  • Reliable: Factually aligned or standard journalistic reporting characteristics.
  • Misleading: Exaggerated, context-lacking, sensationalized, or selective claims.
  • False: Fabricated statements or demonstrably refuted assertions.

2. Explainable AI (Integrated Gradients)

Unlike black-box classifiers that output an opaque confidence score, TruthLens AI uses Integrated Gradients (Sundararajan et al., 2017) to attribute prediction scores back to individual input tokens and phrases.

This highlights specific linguistic cues that pulled the model toward or away from a given veracity class, providing full interpretive transparency for users.

3. Live Evidence Retrieval & Fact-Checking

Language models alone can hallucinate or rely on outdated static weights. TruthLens AI decouples linguistic pattern detection from factual verification by querying live, authoritative external archives:

  • Wikipedia Public API: Extracts encyclopedic fact summaries and context citations.
  • Google Fact Check Tools API: Queries ClaimReview markup published by accredited fact-checkers worldwide.
  • Strict Non-Hallucination Policy: The engine never invents URLs or synthesizes fake source citations. When evidence is insufficient, it explicitly reports INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE.

4. Dual-Signal Verification & Conflict Detection

The verification engine synthesizes both signals:

  • Signal A: Model prediction and token attributions (style and linguistic consistency).
  • Signal B: External evidence retrieval (authoritative corroboration or refutation).

When the two signals diverge (e.g., a claim written in authoritative style that is factually false), TruthLens AI flags a Conflicting Signals Alert, ensuring external facts always supersede internal model confidence.

Responsible AI & Human-in-the-Loop Statement

TruthLens AI is designed as an explainable decision-support system for journalists, researchers, and citizens. Model predictions and retrieved evidence should always be reviewed critically by human decision-makers before drawing final conclusions.
